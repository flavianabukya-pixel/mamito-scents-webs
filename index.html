OUR PAYMENT CHOICES

Why these payment methods?

Mamito Scents sells perfume to everyday customers, so the way we collect money must be easy for you, easy for us to check, and safe for both of us. We tested every method on this page with five questions: Do our customers already use it? Does it work on any phone? Does every payment leave a record we can verify? Is it regulated in Uganda? Does it protect the buyer as well as the seller? These are the methods that passed.

FOR EVERYDAY CUSTOMERS

MTN Mobile Money

MTN is the biggest mobile money network in Uganda, so most of our customers already have an MTN wallet and do not need to open anything new. You pay through *165#, which works on a basic phone with no internet, no smartphone and no bank account. When you enter our number, the phone shows the registered name NABUKYA FLAVIA before you enter your PIN, so you can see you are paying the right person. MTN Mobile Money (U) Ltd is licensed by the Bank of Uganda, and every payment sends you and us an SMS with a Transaction ID that we match in our own MTN account.

FOR EVERYDAY CUSTOMERS

Airtel Money

Customers on Airtel should not have to switch networks just to buy a perfume. MTN and Airtel together handle over 80% of mobile money in Uganda, so offering both means almost anyone with a mobile money wallet can pay with the line they already use, through *185#. It also gives us a second, separate way to receive money, so an order is never stuck because one network is slow. The name check (KIBIRANGO KENNETH), the SMS and the Transaction ID work the same way, so our proof-of-payment steps are identical for both networks.

FOR BUSINESS CUSTOMERS

Bank Transfer

Shops, salons and resellers buy many perfumes at once, and large amounts can run into wallet limits on mobile money. They also need an invoice and a bank record for their own books. That is why bank transfer starts with an invoice on WhatsApp, and why we release the order only when the money shows in our account. A bank reference cannot be faked with an edited screenshot, so it is the safest method for bigger orders.

FOR FIRST-TIME CUSTOMERS

Cash on Delivery

Buying perfume from a new seller online takes trust, and some people simply want to smell and see the product first. With cash on delivery, no money moves before the perfume arrives. You check the item, pay the exact amount to our delivery person and ask for a receipt. It lowers the risk for a first order, and many customers who start this way later move to mobile money.

What we chose not to use, and why

Card payments (not yet)

Taking cards safely needs a licensed payment provider, set-up costs and transaction fees, and many of our customers do not carry bank cards at all. We will add cards only after working with a licensed provider, when enough customers ask for it.

Cryptocurrency (we would not accept it)

The Bank of Uganda has said it has not licensed any institution to sell crypto or help trade it. In April 2022 it told every provider licensed under the National Payment Systems Act, 2020 to stop facilitating crypto transactions, including converting it to mobile money. In April 2023 the High Court dismissed a challenge to that directive. Crypto is also not legal tender in Uganda, its price can change between the day you order and the day we deliver, and a crypto payment cannot be refunded the way our refund note promises. It would put our customers and our business at risk, so we stay with regulated methods.

How it fits together: everyday buyers use MTN or Airtel Money, business buyers use bank transfer, and unsure buyers use cash on delivery. Every method leaves a record we check ourselves, as described in How we confirm your payment, and any refund goes back by the same route you paid.
